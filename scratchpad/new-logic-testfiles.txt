Even when aggressively pared down, fio.ZFS.Ext4.SingleDrive.Benchmark.sh grew
to 288 tests per disk configuration. That's far too much. Even without
accounting for the 10s cache flush, that amounted to 24 hours.

That's within the realm of practicality, but it stretches it, and
it struck me still as rather inefficient.

There were six test types (read write randread randwrite readwrite randrw),
and three ioengines (libaio io_uring psync).

I'm thinking about writing fio test files that have some variables, but may
otherwise remain static for purpose testing (bandwidth, IOPS, latency).

Still, there is some benefit to the exhaustive testing via matrix, if only
to compare limitations of each kind of testing. For example, in manual testing,
I found that ZFS didn't with with varying libaio iodepths.
